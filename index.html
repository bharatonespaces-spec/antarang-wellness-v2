A Safe, Guided Journey

Hello, I'm Sonali Jayantilal Gujarathi. For over a decade, I've had the profound privilege of sitting with individuals and couples as they navigate their most deeply held challenges. My approach isn't about sterile clinical formulas; it's about building a radically safe, empathetic environment where you are truly seen and heard.

As an NCAHP-registered clinical psychologist with foundational experience at esteemed institutions like MGM Hospital and TISS Tuljapur, and serving as an External Member of the ICC under the Ministry of Finance, I bring rigorous, evidence-based expertise to every session. But more importantly, I bring humanity.

With 23 years deeply immersed in theatre and the arts, I carry a unique, nuanced understanding of human expression and emotion. This artistic background allows me to listen to what isn't being said, helping you explore your inner world with grace, creativity, and profound compassion.
